This page provides information about the musical equipment provided in the University Music Club. The aim of this part of the page is to illustrate what students may use during rehearsals, practices, shows, and other activities of the club. Information that will be provided on this page should be relevant to the actual checkup of the musical equipment of the club. In this way, this page may help students in finding out what kind of equipment is provided before attending a rehearsal or performing. The club may have various kinds of musical equipment and audio devices. For this reason, each piece of equipment should be described depending on its actual usage. The photos presented on this page should reflect the actual atmosphere of the club. Below you may find the inventory table of the verified musical equipment. Some pieces of equipment may be used jointly by the students, while others may be intended for particular activities only.

Musical Instruments

The band maintains an assortment of instruments that is small but effective for rehearsals, accompaniment, song writing, and live shows.

Instrument Collection

String Instruments

  • Yamaha F310 acoustic guitar
  • Fender Squier Stratocaster electric guitar

Keyboard Instruments

  • Yamaha PSR-E373 61-key digital keyboard

Percussion Instruments

  • 5-piece standard drum kit with cymbals

Audio Equipment

The club's audio equipment supports rehearsals, recording, communication, and live sound.

Sound Equipment

The following audio equipment may be used by the club: microphones, loudspeakers, mixing desks, amplifiers, cables, etc.

  1. Check the equipment before use.
  2. Connect the required cables carefully.
  3. Test the sound before rehearsal or performance.
  4. Return shared equipment after use.

Equipment Notes

The students are expected to abide by the equipment policy of the Music Club and consult the representative of the Music Club regarding the equipment that needs permission.

For equipment questions, contact the club by email or use the club's real telephone number.

Useful Details

For more information, visit the official Music Club website.

  • Equipment can be booked in advance through the club representative to avoid conflicts during exam season and event weeks.
  • New members are given a short equipment orientation at their first rehearsal.
  • Any malfunction or damage should be reported immediately so it can be repaired before the next session.
  • Personal instruments and gear are welcome, but club equipment always has priority for scheduled rehearsals.
