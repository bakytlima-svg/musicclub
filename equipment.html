This page lists the instruments and sound equipment available to members of the Music Club in rehearsal room C1.1.142. Check it before a rehearsal or a show to see what you can use and what you need to bring yourself. Some items are shared between ensembles, others are intended for particular activities only, so you can book equipment for a given date with the request form at the bottom of the page.

Musical Instruments

The band maintains an assortment of instruments that is small but effective for rehearsals, accompaniment, song writing, and live shows.

Instrument Collection

String Instruments

  • Yamaha F310 acoustic guitar
  • Fender Squier Stratocaster electric guitar

Keyboard Instruments

  • Yamaha PSR-E373 61-key digital keyboard

Percussion Instruments

  • 5-piece standard drum kit with cymbals

Audio Equipment

The club's audio equipment supports rehearsals, recording, communication, and live sound.

Sound Equipment

The following audio equipment may be used by the club: microphones, loudspeakers, mixing desks, amplifiers, cables, etc.

  1. Check the equipment before use.
  2. Connect the required cables carefully.
  3. Test the sound before rehearsal or performance.
  4. Return shared equipment after use.

Equipment Notes

The students are expected to abide by the equipment policy of the Music Club and consult the representative of the Music Club regarding the equipment that needs permission.

For equipment questions, contact the club by email or by phone at +7 (778) 057-32-42.

Useful Details

For more information, follow the club on Instagram.

  • Equipment can be booked in advance with the equipment request form to avoid conflicts during exam season and event weeks.
  • New members are given a short equipment orientation at their first rehearsal.
  • Any malfunction or damage should be reported immediately with the same form so it can be repaired before the next session.
  • Personal instruments and gear are welcome, but club equipment always has priority for scheduled rehearsals.
