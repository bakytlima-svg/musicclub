Educational Sessions & Practical Skill Building

Apart from concert preparation, the AITU Music Club organizes weekly educational workshops for beginner and intermediate musicians. Sessions cover vocal breathing, guitar pedalboard routing, drum tuning, and live mixing inside Room C1.1.142.

During practical workshops, senior members share technical guidance. Our sound engineering mentor notes, Understanding audio equalization is just as crucial as executing a flawless guitar solo. Participants learn to manage signal clipping above 0 dBFS.

What to Bring to Workshops

Instrument players should bring their instruments and 6.35mm jack cables. Vocalists and sound engineering students are provided with microphones and digital mixing consoles.

Audio Concepts & Terminology

Workshop participants learn key audio engineering terms:

Gain Staging

Managing signal levels at each stage to prevent distortion.

Equalization (EQ)

Adjusting frequency ranges to improve audio clarity.

Monitor Feedback

High-pitched resonance caused by amplified sound returning to a microphone.

Terminal Command for Digital Workstation Setup

During recording workshops, students use digital audio workstations (DAW). Press Ctrl + Shift + R to initialize routing. 

daw-control --init --inputs=8
daw-control --set-buffer=128samples
daw-control --start-monitor

[OK] Audio buffers locked at 128 samples. Latency: 2.9ms.
