Educational Sessions & Practical Skill Building

Apart from concert preparation, the AITU Music Club organizes weekly educational workshops for beginner and intermediate musicians. Our educational program covers vocal breathing exercises, guitar pedalboard routing, drum tuning, and live mixing console operation inside Room C1.1.142. These interactive sessions are open to all university students regardless of their current skill level.

During practical workshops, senior members share hands-on technical guidance. As our sound engineering mentor notes, Understanding audio equalization is just as crucial as executing a flawless guitar solo. Participants learn how to manage signal clipping above 0 dBFS and protect sensitive studio equipment. Registration for the upcoming semester workshops is currently open for registration. Reserve your seat with the registration form below the timetable.

What to Bring to Workshops

Acoustic and electric instrument players should bring their own instruments and 6.35mm jack cables. Vocalists and sound engineering students are provided with dynamic microphones and digital mixing consoles on site.

Audio Concepts & Terminology

Workshop participants master key audio engineering terms used in live stage environments:

Gain Staging

The process of managing signal levels at each stage of amplification to prevent harmonic distortion.

Equalization (EQ)

Adjusting specific frequency ranges to improve clarity between vocalists and instruments.

Monitor Feedback

High-pitched acoustic resonance caused when amplified sound loops back into a live microphone.

Terminal Command for Digital Workstation Setup

During recording workshops, students use digital audio workstations (DAW). Press Ctrl + Shift + R on the control room PC to initialize driver routing: 

daw-control --init --inputs=8
daw-control --set-buffer=128samples
daw-control --start-monitor

The studio terminal will return the following status message:

[OK] Audio buffers locked at 128 samples. Latency: 2.9ms.
