The University Music Club unites students for rehearsals, performances and other musical collaborations. This page introduces the student bands and performers connected with the club — their genres, members, instruments, and experience of performance. Group styles range from acoustic and pop to rock, jazz and electronic music, and the lineup shifts throughout the academic year as students join, graduate, form new groups or prepare for performance. Here you can learn about the current student groups, and see what musical activities may be of interest to you.

Student Groups

NEON PULSE

Genre: K-POP
Members: Arujan, Jibek, Yerasyl, Dinmuhammed, Arsen, Vlad
Instruments: Vocals, Electric guitar, Bass guitar, Drum kit, Keyboard

A five-member group combining striking vocals, synchronized choreography, and energetic rhythms. They rehearse twice a week and prepare performances for club shows and student events.

NEON PULSE at a Music Club activity.

MOLDIK

Genre: Lyrics
Members: Yersultан, Meruert, Dankibek, Asem, Sherkhan
Instruments: Vocals, Electric guitar, Bass guitar, Drum kit, Keyboard

A five-member group focused on original lyrics and live guitar-based performances. Their sets are built around stories and personal experiences and are frequently performed at the club's open mic nights.

MOLDIK at a Music Club performance.
