Joining Our Campus Ensembles

At the beginning of each academic semester, the Astana IT University Music Club hosts auditions for newly enrolled and returning students. We are looking for passionate vocalists, guitarists, bassists, drummers, keyboard players, and brass instrument performers. Tryouts take place in our main rehearsal space in Room C1.1.142 between 15:00 and 20:00.

Our audition committee evaluates rhythm stability, pitch control, and willingness to collaborate within team ensembles. Candidates are asked to prepare two short musical fragments: one primary solo piece and one impromptu rhythm test. Remember that stage confidence matters more than flawless speed. Current recruitment is active for all performance tracks.

Applicants should bring their student ID and arrive early enough to prepare before the audition.

Handling Stage Nerves

Arrive 15 minutes before your time slot to warm up your hands or vocal cords. Our mentors foster an encouraging atmosphere, so treat the tryout as a collaborative jam session rather than an exam.

Selection Stages & Repertoire Categories

Four-Step Recruitment Workflow

  1. Fill out and submit the official applicant registration form below.
  2. Receive time-slot confirmation via your student email address.
  3. Perform your prepared 3-minute piece before the evaluation panel.
  4. Participate in a short 5-minute group rhythm exercise with fellow applicants.

Auditioned Instrument Categories

Rhythm and Bass Section

  • 4-String and 5-String Bass Guitars
  • Acoustic Drums and Percussion Pads

Melodic & Lead Instruments

  • Electric Lead Guitar and Rhythm Guitar
  • Synthesizers and Digital Piano
  • Saxophone, Trumpet, and Violin

Performance Focus

Candidates can demonstrate their musical skills through prepared pieces, rhythm exercises and ensemble work.
